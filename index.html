About

I am an agricultural engineer at BRRI, working on farm mechanization and postharvest technology for rice. My work covers the design, fabrication and field evaluation of small and medium machines, including rice transplanting, mechanical weeding, harvesting, threshing, milling and the use of rice husk as an energy source.

More recently I have joined climate focused work on gridded precipitation and temperature data and on rice exposure to heat stress in Bangladesh under different scenarios.
